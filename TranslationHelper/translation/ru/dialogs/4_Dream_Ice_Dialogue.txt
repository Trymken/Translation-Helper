4_Dream_Ice Dialogue

NOTES:
- sad-ish/melancholy worldly type level (dialogue mostly, not really any platforming)
- should add in mountainous/cliffy area near where cave is for platforming challenge
- lots of sad homeless NPCs giving their two cents, people keep commenting on how no ones lived in that last building (eluding to a ghost)
- whole concept is you run into a ghostly-looking guy wholed up in a building who says he wants you to find his lost dog
- you can't progress anywhere before this due to a fence blocking the way (near fallen billboard)
- fence disappears after talking to ghost guy
- must climb mountain to find cave on top where his dog is living
- the dog will not listen to you unless you have a treat to give (he tells you this before hand) (you must get the treat from another NPC after getting dog quest)
- treat NPC is the fishing guy beneath the tower
- once you get the dog to go back, you go talk again to ghostly NPC and he disappears after thanking you. The dream shard is where he last was
