8 Dream Tower of Heaven

Notes
- Not sure if Gogi mimic is appropriate here, might just have him scamper off when approached
- Whole concept is to climb to the top of a massive set(?) of towers
- there are hooded devil creatures near the lower parts of the towers that give depressing dialogue
- at a certain point you start running into Gatekeeper-like creatures that offer uplifting dialogue
- there are remnants of past dreams floating in the ocean in the background
- at the very top of the last tower is a giant Gatekeeper with wings with the last dream crystal shard in front of it
- the mood/music/ambience change once you get to a certain point
- respawn zones will adjust as you go higher and higher
- is this too preachy??
